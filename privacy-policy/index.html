Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application “Scavenger Hunt Kids – Pin Publisher” (the “App”) handles data. The App is a private, single-user tool operated by its owner to publish their own product pins to their own Pinterest business account via the Pinterest API.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by the owner of the Scavenger Hunt Kids product line (the “Operator”). Contact: scavanger.hunt.kids@proton.me.

2. Relationship to Pinterest

This App and the Scavenger Hunt Kids website are not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Pinterest in any way. “Pinterest” is a trademark of Pinterest, Inc. The App merely uses the public Pinterest API to let the Operator publish content to the Operator’s own account.

3. What data the App accesses

The App connects only to the Operator’s own Pinterest business account using Pinterest OAuth. It uses this access solely to create boards and pins owned by the Operator. The App does not access, collect, or process data belonging to any other Pinterest user.

4. What data the App stores

The App stores a Pinterest OAuth access token locally, on the Operator’s own computer, only for the purpose of authenticating API requests. No user data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. The App does not collect personal data from visitors or end users.

5. Third-party services

The App interacts with the Pinterest API. Use of Pinterest is governed by Pinterest’s own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Disconnection, data retention, and deletion

The only Pinterest-derived data the App holds is the locally stored OAuth access token. When the Operator disconnects the App — either by revoking access from the Pinterest account settings or by deleting the local token file — that token is invalidated and removed, and the App retains no Pinterest-derived data whatsoever. The App keeps no separate database, backup, or copy of any Pinterest content or Pinterest user data after disconnection.

7. No resale or redistribution

The Operator does not sell, license, rent, or otherwise redistribute Pinterest content or any Pinterest-derived data to any third party. All API access is used exclusively to publish the Operator’s own product pins to the Operator’s own Pinterest account.

8. Children’s privacy

The App does not collect any data from children. The products it promotes are printable activities intended for families; the App itself processes no end-user data.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to scavanger.hunt.kids@proton.me.
